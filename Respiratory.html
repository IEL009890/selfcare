Care Tips

Practice deep breathing exercises to strengthen lung capacity and improve oxygen intake.

Avoid exposure to pollutants, allergens, and secondhand smoke to reduce inflammation.

Maintaining a healthy weight helps prevent strain on your lungs and reduces the risk of respiratory problems.
